TabSnap

When you close the tab you're on, Chrome usually just jumps to the tab on its right.
TabSnap picks a better one: the nearest tab you actually used, or the one you were just on.

Install in 3 steps

  1. Download the zip and unzip it. You'll get a tabsnap folder.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the tabsnap folder. Click the ⚡ icon in the toolbar to choose a strategy.

Works in Chrome and other Chromium browsers (Edge, Brave, Arc). It isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet.

The three strategies

Proximity

Go to the neighbour of the closed tab. If both the left and right neighbour qualify, go to the one you used more recently.

LRU

Go back to the tab you used most recently, wherever it is in the tab bar.

Hybrid

Proximity, unless the neighbour hasn't been used for over 5 minutes. Then go back to your most recent tab.

Privacy

TabSnap only asks for the tabs and storage permissions. It has no content scripts, makes no network requests, and stores tab-use timestamps only on your machine. Your strategy choice syncs through your Chrome profile.
